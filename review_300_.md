## Interest of content
Every topic covered in these pages is of relevance and interest. I found communications and medicine particularly intriguing; regardless, if any topic was to grab your attentino, numerous links and references to further reading were included.

## Consistency and quality of visual representation
Every page includes the same representation of a black background and green text. Spacing between content is regular across pages, and applications of headers and larger text to signify information is universal. However, with the exception of the AI page, little use of the main stylesheet is used, which detracts from the visual quality of the page.

## Quality of written presentation
As previously stated, every page provides extensive reference to further reading if desired, and gives you images to provide context for the topic. However, the content immediately available on the page on the communications, medicine and education pages is easy to read quickly through, leaving you wanting. This problem is not evident in the page about AI.

## Validity of HTML
Generally, no particularly noticeable problems were found in the HTML. Admirable effort was made in adhering to XML syntax rules. Despite this, a couple of flaws are still present - the AI page includes a presumably unintentional overlap between pictures, and the communications page makes incorrect use of the ul tag.

## Evidence of collaboration
The maintenance of a consistent visual theme is obvious throughout each page, and the links to further information in each speak to a clear collaboration between group members. This is also indicated by the commits, where most members have a mix of commits and total changes to the repository.
