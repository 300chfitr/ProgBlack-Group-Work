Access to education

Intro

Developments in computing and digital technology have brought an age of unprecidented access to education. Allowing more people to develop skills, think more critcally and better improve the communities they live in.

Covid-19

During the Covid-19 pandemic, lockdown meant that students, teachers, professors and all other education staff could not attend school. Digital technologies played a crutial role in ensuring that students still had access to an education, with video conferencing software such as Zoom and Google meet allowing for educaters host remote online classes from the cofort of their own homes. Apps such as Microsoft Teams and google classroom also provided a digital environment for educators to set assignments for their students, veiw students' work and communicate with students outside of the online classes.

Access to education around the world

Advancements in computing and digital technology have lead to an increased access to education around the globe. The growth of the internet, increased availability of computers including mobile devices means that education is no longer confined to a physical classroom. This has allowed pupils around the world to access high quality educational material that they would not be able to access traditionally. This is especially useful in areas which lack proper educational infrastructure so populations can get an improved education, through online learing platforms, massive open online courses (MOOCS), online tests, subject papers etc etc.
